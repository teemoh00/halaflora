Company Profile

Hala Flora is a Saudi–Kenyan company specializing in the export of premium fresh-cut flowers. Combining Saudi business expertise with Kenya’s world-renowned flower industry, we are committed to delivering exceptional quality flowers to customers across the Gulf region, the Arab world, and international markets.

We work closely with trusted flower farms and certified growers in Kenya to ensure every shipment meets the highest standards of freshness, quality, and sustainability. Through efficient cold-chain logistics and reliable air freight, we guarantee that our flowers arrive in perfect condition.


Our Promise

At Hala Flora, every flower represents our commitment to freshness, beauty, and excellence. We take pride in connecting the finest Kenyan flowers with customers around the world through dependable service and uncompromising quality.
