Company Profile

Hala Flora is a Saudi–Kenyan company specializing in the export of premium fresh-cut flowers. Combining Saudi business expertise with Kenya’s world-renowned flower industry, we deliver exceptional quality flowers to customers across the Gulf region and global markets.

We work closely with trusted flower farms and certified growers in Kenya to ensure every shipment meets the highest standards of freshness, quality, and sustainability.
